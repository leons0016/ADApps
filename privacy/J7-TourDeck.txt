PRIVACY POLICY - TourDeck and TourDeck Plus (JetBrains IDE plugins)

1. Summary

TourDeck and TourDeck Plus work on your computer. They do not collect or transmit any data. They have no accounts, no analytics, no
advertising, no telemetry and make no network connection of their own. The tours you write are files in your own project.

2. What the plugins do with your data

- TourDeck reads and writes tour files in the folder .tours of your project directory (files ending in .tour or .tour.md). A tour file
  contains the title and text you wrote, and for each step the path of a file inside the project, line numbers, the name of a class or
  method, and up to three lines of code text taken from that place (used to find the place again when the code moves). Tour files are
  ordinary project files: they are shared only if you commit and push them yourself.
- The recorder remembers, in memory, the places you stop at in the editor while you record. Nothing is written until you confirm the
  review dialog.
- To open a step, TourDeck reads the file the step points to, like the editor does. It reads the folder .git of your project (the
  files HEAD, packed-refs and the branch files) to note the current commit in a new tour; it does not run git for this.
- Your progress through tours (which step you reached, whether you finished a tour) is saved in the workspace file of the project in
  the .idea folder of your project, on your computer. It is not part of the tour files and is not shared unless you share your .idea
  workspace file.
- TourDeck Plus compares tour steps with the files of your project. With the Git plugin of your IDE it runs the commands git diff,
  git blame, git rev-parse and git cat-file on your local repository to find where lines moved and who last changed them. The results
  are shown in the IDE and kept in memory; the author name and date from git blame are shown to you and are not stored by the plugin.
  Exporting the checklist or the stale-step list copies text to your clipboard when you press the button.
- The plugins contain no analytics library, no crash-reporting service and no "check for updates" call.

3. Data stored on your computer

- Application settings in the IDE configuration folder (options/tourdeck.xml and options/tourdeck-plus.xml): gutter marks, recorder timing,
  staleness options.
- In the workspace file of the project: your progress through tours and, with Plus, the role you chose.
- In the project: the tour files in .tours that you create.
Uninstalling the plugins does not remove tour files or progress.

4. Licences and purchases

Sales, payments, invoices, taxes and licence keys are handled by JetBrains Marketplace under JetBrains' own terms and privacy policy.
The plugins only read the signed licence confirmation that the IDE already holds, through the official IDE licensing API, and check its
signature locally. They do not send licence information anywhere. The vendor receives sales reports from JetBrains but does not receive
your personal data from the plugins.

5. Error reports

If the IDE shows an error from a plugin you may choose "Report by Email". This only opens your own mail client with a draft that contains
the plugin name and version, the stack trace and any text you typed, with operating-system user names removed from paths. Nothing is
sent unless you send the email yourself, and you can edit or delete the draft first.

6. Children

The plugins are developer tools and are not directed at children.

7. Your rights

Because the vendor does not receive personal data from the plugins, there is no personal data to access, correct or delete. If you sent
the vendor an email, you can ask for your email to be deleted by writing to the contact address above.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the URL above with a new effective date.
