StatusLedger - Privacy Policy
Publisher: LeonS
Contact: atulhritik@hotmail.com
Applies to the Apify Actor "Status Page Monitor - StatusLedger".
Public address of this text: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/leons0016/ADApps/main/privacy/P6-StatusLedger.txt

1. What the Actor processes
- Public status information that vendors publish on their own status pages: page and component names and states, incident titles, severity, start and end times and the text of incident updates.
- The data you give the Actor: your list of status pages, your settings (look-back, SLA basis and credit tiers, vendor names and monthly fees you enter) and, if you set them, a webhook address and a Slack webhook address.
- The Actor does not collect personal data about people. Incident texts are written by the vendors; if a vendor puts a person's name in an update, it is copied as published.

2. Where the data lives
- Your input, results and ledger stay in your own Apify account: the run's dataset and key-value store, and the named key-value store "leons-statusledger-<ledger name>-ledger". The publisher does not receive them and runs no server that stores them.

3. Retention
- The ledger is kept until you delete the named store. Nothing is deleted by the Actor, because the ledger is meant to be a permanent record. Datasets and key-value stores of runs follow the retention rules of your Apify plan.

4. Billing and processors
- Apify bills you and acts as the processor for billing and for running the Actor. The Actor has no payment or account data of its own.

5. Sources, politeness and secrets
- The Actor reads public status pages only, checks robots.txt for every host and never requests an address that robots.txt disallows, identifies itself in its User-Agent, does not log in, does not solve CAPTCHAs, and uses no browser and no proxy.
- The webhook and Slack webhook addresses are stored as secret input and are never written to logs, datasets or reports.

6. Purpose and legal note
- The data is used to record vendor incidents and to estimate monthly uptime. The figures are estimates from what vendors published and are not a contractual measurement or legal advice.

7. Your rights and questions
- Write to atulhritik@hotmail.com to ask about this policy or to report a data problem.
