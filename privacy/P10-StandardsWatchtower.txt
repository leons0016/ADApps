Standards Watchtower - Privacy Policy
Publisher: LeonS
Contact: atulhritik@hotmail.com
Applies to the Apify Actor "Standards Revision Monitor - Standards Watchtower".
Public address of this text: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/leons0016/ADApps/main/privacy/P10-StandardsWatchtower.txt

1. What the Actor processes
- Public metadata about standards that the standards body publishes: designation, title, stage, dates, committee, classification codes and the links between documents (supersedes, superseded by, amendments). Never the text of a standard.
- The data you give the Actor: your list of designations, your settings (watchlist name, draft tracking, whether the public ISO page of changed standards is read) and, if you set them, a webhook address and a Slack webhook address.
- The Actor does not collect personal data about people. The data it reads describes documents, not persons.

2. Where the data lives
- Your input, results and the last known stage of each designation stay in your own Apify account: the run's dataset and key-value store, and the named key-value store "leons-standardswatchtower-state". The publisher does not receive them and runs no server that stores them.

3. Retention
- The state of a designation is kept in your named store until you delete the store; a designation that is no longer checked is dropped from the state after 400 days. Datasets and key-value stores of runs follow the retention rules of your Apify plan.

4. Billing and processors
- Apify bills you and acts as the processor for billing and for running the Actor. The Actor has no payment or account data of its own.

5. Sources, politeness and secrets
- The Actor downloads the ISO Open Data file "iso_deliverables_metadata" (licence: ODC Attribution License v1.0) once per run. If you switch the option on, it also reads the public ISO page of designations that changed, at most 25 per run and one page every 5 seconds, after checking robots.txt. It does not log in, does not solve CAPTCHAs, uses no browser and no proxy, and identifies itself in its User-Agent.
- The webhook and Slack webhook addresses are stored as secret input and are never written to logs, datasets or reports.

6. Purpose and legal note
- The data is used to record the stage of the standards you watch and to report when it changes. The information is what the standards body published. It is not legal advice and does not replace the standards body's own record. Standards Watchtower is not affiliated with or endorsed by ISO.

7. Your rights and questions
- Write to atulhritik@hotmail.com to ask about this policy or to report a data problem.
