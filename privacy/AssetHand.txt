AssetHand Privacy Policy

Publisher: LeonS, India
Contact: atulhritik@hotmail.com
Effective date: 30 October 2026

1. What AssetHand is
AssetHand is a plug-in for Autodesk Revit that reads the elements, rooms, levels and parameters of your model and writes COBie 2.4 asset-handover workbooks (XLSX and CSV), with a validator, client mapping templates, delta export and an export audit log.

2. Information we collect
We do not collect, receive or store any of your project data, model content, drawings, spreadsheets, names, files or personal information on our servers. AssetHand has no account system and no analytics.

3. Information stored on your computer and in your model
- AssetHand settings that belong to a project are stored inside your Revit model file, so they travel with the file: the project set-up (facility, project and site details and the contact list, which can include names, email addresses, phone numbers and postal addresses that you type in), your client mapping templates, the export audit log and the issue snapshots used for delta export. The audit log contains, for each recorded export, the Revit user name of the person who exported, the date and time, the template name, the file name (not the folder), the counts of rows, errors and warnings, and the names of the models included. The snapshots contain one fingerprint value per exported row, not the row contents. All of this can be removed with the "Remove all AssetHand data from this model" button in the ? guide. AssetHand also writes shared parameters (names starting with COBie.) to your model elements when you ask it to; these are ordinary Revit parameters that you can delete in Revit.
- Your personal settings (the last export folder) are stored in %AppData%\LeonS\AssetHand on your computer.
- Files you export (XLSX, CSV) and template files you export are saved only where you choose. They contain the data from your model and the contacts you entered, and you are responsible for who receives them.
- A small encrypted licence cache and your trial start time are stored on your computer in your Windows user area (%LocalAppData%\LeonS\Licensing and the Windows registry key HKEY_CURRENT_USER\Software\LeonS\assethand). The cache holds only a one-way hash of your Autodesk user id and the dates of the last licence checks.
- Log files, which contain technical messages and element ids but never model content, contact details, your user id or file names, are stored in %LocalAppData%\LeonS\AssetHand\logs and are deleted automatically after 14 days.
We never read these files remotely.

4. Network use
AssetHand contacts one service: the Autodesk App Store licence service (apps.autodesk.com), to check whether your signed-in Autodesk account has purchased AssetHand or subscribed to its add-on. This request carries your Autodesk user id and the application identifier, as required by Autodesk, and is handled under Autodesk's own privacy statement. We do not receive the result or the request. AssetHand makes no other network connections.

5. Sharing and third parties
We do not sell, share or disclose information, because we do not receive any. The only third party involved is Autodesk (licence check, section 4); Autodesk handles that request under its own privacy statement and data protection commitments.

6. Children
AssetHand is professional software and is not directed at children.

7. Retention, deletion and consent
- Data inside your model stays until you delete it. Local files stay until you delete them (the list is in section 3). Log files are deleted automatically after 14 days.
- AssetHand does not ask for consent to collect personal data because it collects none. If you do not want the licence check to run, sign out of Autodesk in Revit or uninstall AssetHand; the 24-hour trial and purchased features then stop working as described in the licence terms, and you can revoke this at any time by signing in again.
- To request deletion of anything we hold about you, write to atulhritik@hotmail.com. We hold no personal data, and will confirm that in writing.

8. Your choices
You can delete all AssetHand data from a model with the "Remove all AssetHand data from this model" button in the ? guide. You can delete the local files listed in section 3 at any time; AssetHand will then treat the computer as new (the 24-hour trial is not restarted for a previously started trial on the same Windows user, and purchases are restored by signing in to Autodesk).

9. Changes
If this policy changes, the new text is published with the next version of AssetHand on the Autodesk App Store, with a new effective date.
