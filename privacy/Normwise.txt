Normwise Privacy Policy

Publisher: LeonS, India
Contact: atulhritik@hotmail.com
Effective date: 30 October 2026

1. What Normwise is
Normwise is a plug-in for Autodesk Revit that compares an open project with an office standards file (view templates, view filters, line patterns and styles, text and dimension types, browser organisations), shows the difference, and copies the items you choose into the project. Optional add-on features save a compliance report, a CSV file of the difference, and a re-check reminder.

2. Information we collect
We do not collect, receive or store any of your project data, model content, drawings, files or personal information on our servers. Normwise has no account system and no analytics.

3. Information stored on your computer and in your model
- The only data Normwise stores in a Revit model is the re-check reminder (add-on): how often to re-check, the path of the standards file you chose, and the date and result of the last check. It sits on the Project Information element, so it travels with the file, and it can be removed with the "Remove all Normwise data from this model" button in the ? guide. Standards you push into a project are ordinary Revit elements (view templates, filters, types) and stay in the model like any other.
- Your personal settings (last standards file, list of properties to ignore, last export folder) are stored in %AppData%\LeonS\Normwise on your computer.
- The standards file you choose is opened read-only in the background through the Revit API, is never saved or modified, and is closed when you leave the Normwise window. If the file is workshared it is detached in memory and the central model is not touched. Nothing from it is kept after the window is closed.
- Reports and CSV files you save are written only where you choose. They contain names and settings of standards items and the project name, and you are responsible for who receives them.
- A small encrypted licence cache and your trial start time are stored on your computer in your Windows user area (%LocalAppData%\LeonS\Licensing and the Windows registry key HKEY_CURRENT_USER\Software\LeonS\normwise). The cache holds only a one-way hash of your Autodesk user id and the dates of the last licence checks.
- Log files, which contain technical messages but never model content, your user id or file names, are stored in %LocalAppData%\LeonS\Normwise\logs and are deleted automatically after 14 days.
We never read these files remotely.

4. Network use
Normwise contacts one service: the Autodesk App Store licence service (apps.autodesk.com), to check whether your signed-in Autodesk account has purchased Normwise or subscribed to its add-on. This request carries your Autodesk user id and the application identifier, as required by Autodesk, and is handled under Autodesk's own privacy statement. We do not receive the result or the request. Normwise makes no other network connections.

5. Sharing and third parties
We do not sell, share or disclose information, because we do not receive any. The only third party involved is Autodesk (licence check, section 4); Autodesk handles that request under its own privacy statement and data protection commitments.

6. Children
Normwise is professional software and is not directed at children.

7. Retention, deletion and consent
- Data inside your model stays until you delete it. Local files stay until you delete them (the list is in section 3). Log files are deleted automatically after 14 days.
- Normwise does not ask for consent to collect personal data because it collects none. If you do not want the licence check to run, sign out of Autodesk in Revit or uninstall Normwise; the 24-hour trial and purchased features then stop working as described in the licence terms, and you can revoke this at any time by signing in again.
- To request deletion of anything we hold about you, write to atulhritik@hotmail.com. We hold no personal data, and will confirm that in writing.

8. Your choices
You can delete the reminder from a model with the "Remove all Normwise data from this model" button in the ? guide. You can delete the local files listed in section 3 at any time; Normwise will then treat the computer as new (the 24-hour trial is not restarted for a previously started trial on the same Windows user, and purchases are restored by signing in to Autodesk).

9. Changes
If this policy changes, the new text is published with the next version of Normwise on the Autodesk App Store, with a new effective date.
