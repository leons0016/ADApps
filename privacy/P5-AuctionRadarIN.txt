1. What the Actor does
AuctionRadar IN reads public auction notices published by the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India (ibbi.gov.in) and the State Bank of India (sbi.bank.in) and returns them as structured rows to the Apify account that runs it.

2. Data the Actor keeps
Per auction: source, notice id, company or firm name of the borrower or corporate debtor, name of the insolvency professional (a public professional role), bank and branch, asset description, property address, city, district, state, pincode, area, reserve price, EMD, bid increment, auction and inspection dates, possession type, business e-mail address or landline of the bank branch or professional's office, links to the notice.

3. Individuals
Borrower names that look like individuals are replaced with "[Individual borrower]" by default. The user can switch this off. Guarantor and co-borrower names, mobile telephone numbers, personal e-mail addresses and borrower residence addresses are never output or stored.

4. Where data lives
The results, the memory of already delivered auctions (a hash, a timestamp and the auction date and reserve price per auction) and the reports live in the user's own Apify account. The publisher runs no server and receives no customer data. The publisher may run a shared cache of parsed public notices in the publisher's own Apify account; it never contains individual borrower names.

5. Retention
Memory entries are removed 60 days after the auction date. Cache entries are kept for auctions dated within the last 30 days.

6. Removal requests
Write to atulhritik@hotmail.com with the link to the notice. The publisher removes the auction from the shared cache within 7 days.

7. Not advice
Information from public auction notices. Verify all details with the bank or insolvency professional and the official notice before bidding. Not investment or legal advice.

8. Changes
Changes to this policy are published at the address above.
