RebarIN Privacy Policy

Publisher: LeonS, India
Contact: atulhritik@hotmail.com
Effective date: 24 October 2026

1. What RebarIN is
RebarIN is a plug-in for Autodesk Revit that adds IS 2502 rebar shapes and creates bar bending schedules from the rebar in your model.

2. Information we collect
We do not collect, receive or store any of your project data, drawings, rebar, schedules, names, files or personal information on our servers. RebarIN has no account system and no analytics.

3. Information stored on your computer and in your model
- RebarIN settings that belong to a project are stored inside your Revit model file, so they travel with the file: the project rule set, the shape code stored on shapes RebarIN created, and the settings of each bar bending schedule view. They can be removed with the "Remove all RebarIN data from this model" button in the ? guide. RebarIN also writes shared parameters (names starting with RIN_) and their values to your rebar; these are ordinary Revit parameters that you can delete in Revit.
- Your personal rule sets are stored in %AppData%\LeonS\RebarIN on your computer. If you use the add-on feature "Firm rules", rule files are also written to the shared folder that you choose.
- Files you export (XLSX) are saved only where you choose.
- A small encrypted licence cache and your trial start time are stored on your computer in your Windows user area (%LocalAppData%\LeonS\Licensing and the Windows registry key HKEY_CURRENT_USER\Software\LeonS\rebarin). The cache holds only a one-way hash of your Autodesk user id and the dates of the last licence checks.
- Log files, which contain technical messages and element ids but never model content, your user id or file names, are stored in %LocalAppData%\LeonS\RebarIN\logs and are deleted automatically after 14 days.
We never read these files remotely.

4. Network use
RebarIN contacts one service: the Autodesk App Store licence service (apps.autodesk.com), to check whether your signed-in Autodesk account has purchased RebarIN or subscribed to its add-on. This request carries your Autodesk user id and the application identifier, as required by Autodesk, and is handled under Autodesk's own privacy statement. We do not receive the result or the request. RebarIN makes no other network connections.

5. Sharing and third parties
We do not sell, share or disclose information, because we do not receive any. The only third party involved is Autodesk (licence check, section 4); Autodesk handles that request under its own privacy statement and data protection commitments.

6. Children
RebarIN is professional software and is not directed at children.

7. Retention, deletion and consent
- Data inside your model stays until you delete it. Local files stay until you delete them (the list is in section 3). Log files are deleted automatically after 14 days.
- RebarIN does not ask for consent to collect personal data because it collects none. If you do not want the licence check to run, sign out of Autodesk in Revit or uninstall RebarIN; the 24-hour trial and purchased features then stop working as described in the licence terms, and you can revoke this at any time by signing in again.
- To request deletion of anything we hold about you, write to atulhritik@hotmail.com. We hold no personal data, and will confirm that in writing.

8. Your choices
You can delete all RebarIN data from a model with the "Remove all RebarIN data from this model" button in the ? guide. You can delete the local files listed in section 3 at any time; RebarIN will then treat the computer as new (the 24-hour trial is not restarted for a previously started trial on the same Windows user, and purchases are restored by signing in to Autodesk).

9. Changes
If this policy changes, the new text is published with the next version of RebarIN on the Autodesk App Store, with a new effective date.
