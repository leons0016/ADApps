| App | What it does | Help | Privacy | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **ScopeShape** (Revit 2025-2027) | Polygon zones used as the crop region of every view you choose | [Help](help/ScopeShape.md) | [Privacy](privacy/ScopeShape.txt) | [Terms](eula/ScopeShape.txt) |

The privacy policies and licence terms are plain text.
