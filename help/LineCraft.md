# LineCraft help

Text, symbols and true dots on any Revit line, curves included, that update when you edit. Works in Revit 2025, 2026 and 2027.

## Getting started
1. Install LineCraft from the Autodesk App Store, start Revit and open a project. A **LineCraft** tab appears on the ribbon. It is free for 24 hours from the first start; after that a one-time purchase unlocks it.
2. Open the **Styles pane**. It lists 20 ready-made styles: gas, water, sewer, storm, electric, telecom, fibre, chilled water, fire main, fence X and O, 1 HR and 2 HR fire rating, smoke barrier, setback, easement, true dots, tree line, overhead power, railway.
3. **Apply a style** to selected detail lines (straight lines, arcs, ellipses, splines, or several joined lines as one chain), or choose a style and draw new lines with Revit's own line tool. Labels sit on the line, read upright, keep clear of corners and hide the line under them.
4. **Edit freely.** Drag, move, mirror, copy or change the view scale: labels and symbols follow automatically.
5. **Override and release.** Change the text for one chain, release a chain back to a plain line, refresh a view or the whole project, or remove all LineCraft data.
6. **Make your own styles** in the style editor with a live preview. Save them to your library or inside the project.

Everything LineCraft draws is a normal Revit detail line, text note or filled region, so models open and print the same without LineCraft.

## Optional add-on (the "Try add on feature" panel)
- **Import AutoCAD .lin** linetypes as LineCraft styles.
- **Audit window** listing every LineCraft chain, with CSV export.
- **Batch convert** every line of a plain Revit line style into a LineCraft style.

## The ? button
The **?** button on the Help panel opens the full in-app guide: every feature, each step and where to find it, plus plans, licence, privacy and data topics.

## Removing data
Use **Remove all LineCraft data** in the guide. Lines already drawn stay in the model as normal Revit elements.
