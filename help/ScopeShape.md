# ScopeShape help

Draw any zone shape once. Every assigned view gets the crop and stays in sync. Works in Revit 2025, 2026 and 2027.

## Getting started
1. Install ScopeShape from the Autodesk App Store, start Revit and open a project. A **ScopeShape** tab appears on the ribbon. It is free for 24 hours from the first start; after that a one-time purchase unlocks it.
2. **Make a zone.** Sketch the corners in a plan view, build one from a closed loop of detail lines, turn a view's non-rectangular crop into a zone, or convert a scope box.
3. **Assign views.** Open the **Zone matrix**: every plan view is listed against its zone, with filters and bulk assignment. Each assigned view gets the zone outline as its crop region (floor, ceiling, structural, area and dependent views).
4. **Edit once, update everywhere.** Edit a zone's outline with Revit's own line tools and finish. All assigned views update in one undo step; the model stays in the same place on the sheet.
5. **Sync check.** Finds crops that were changed by hand. For each one choose Fix, Accept or Ignore.
6. **Dependent views.** One dependent view per zone in one click.
7. **Export / import zones** between projects, and an **audit CSV** of every view.

## Optional add-on (the "Try add on feature" panel)
- **Key plan:** every zone drawn as a coloured, labelled hatch in a view you choose, redrawn automatically.
- **Auto-assign rules:** rules such as "floor plans on levels 2 to 9 using template A-Plan get zone East"; new views are matched and cropped for you.

## The ? button
The **?** button on the Help panel opens the full in-app guide: every feature, each step and where to find it, plus plans, licence, privacy and data topics.

## Removing data
Use **Remove all ScopeShape data** in the guide. Crops already applied stay in the model as normal Revit crop regions.
